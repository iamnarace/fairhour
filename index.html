Times are scored in each person’s own hours: before or after work costs 1 point an hour, weekends 2, night 5. fairhour picks the rotation that keeps the worst-off person’s total lowest.

The plan

fairhour runs entirely in your browser using your device’s time-zone data. Plans are saved on this device; shared links carry the plan in the part of the address that is never sent to a server. Source on GitHub
